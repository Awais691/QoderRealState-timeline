    A valuation should tell you something you did not already know.

    Not a number designed to win your listing — a considered view of your home, its street and the buyers most likely to value it.

    No obligation Walkthrough in person Five landscapes

    The approach

    Price is a story
    about context.

    Two houses can look alike on paper and feel entirely different in person. A useful valuation begins with the things a spreadsheet cannot see.

    We look at comparable sales, certainly. But we also look at light, proportion, noise, outlook, the walk to the corner and the way a street changes over a day. Then we talk about the buyers who would notice those things.

    The result is a pricing conversation with reasons — and a plan for presenting the home that does not rely on erasing what makes it yours.

    01

    Walked, not estimated

    Every fictional valuation in this demo begins with an in-person walkthrough and an honest conversation about condition.

    02

    Written, not implied

    You leave with a clear summary of the reasoning, the recommended range and the trade-offs behind it.

    1:1 The advisor who values your home also represents it

    The process

    Four steps,
    no surprises.

    A small practice can move quickly without rushing the parts that matter. This is the process we describe in the demo.

    01

    Conversation

    A short call to understand your timing, your questions and what you want from the sale.

    02

    Walkthrough

    We visit in person, take our time and talk through condition, presentation and likely buyer profiles.

    03

    Strategy

    You receive a written pricing range, a preparation plan and a clear view of the trade-offs.

    04

    Launch

    Photography, editorial copy and a measured release — handled by the advisor you have been working with.
