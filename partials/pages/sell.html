The approach

Price is a story
about context.

Two houses can look alike on paper and feel entirely different in person. A useful valuation begins with the things a spreadsheet cannot see.

We look at comparable sales, certainly. But we also look at light, proportion, noise, outlook, the walk to the corner and the way a street changes over a day. Then we talk about the buyers who would notice those things.

The result is a pricing conversation with reasons — and a plan for presenting the home that does not rely on erasing what makes it yours.
