## 6. Micro-Interactions and Mini Animations

Add subtle, polished motion that improves the experience rather than distracting from it.

Required interactions:
- Smooth scrolling where appropriate.
- Subtle hero content entrance animation.
- Gentle image zoom on property card hover.
- Smooth button hover and active states.
- Underline or color transitions for navigation links.
- Small dropdown and mobile-menu transitions.
- Property card reveal animations when entering the viewport.
- Smooth carousel movement where applicable.
- Favorite/heart button toggle.
- Filter panel interactions.
- Form validation feedback.
- Subtle header transition when scrolling.
- Accessible accordion animations.
- Small success feedback after frontend-only form validation.

Animation principles:
- Use short, refined transitions, generally around 150–350 ms for hover and interface feedback.
- Avoid excessive parallax, bouncing effects, constant floating objects, and unnecessary animation.
- Avoid making every section animate identically.
- Respect the prefers-reduced-motion accessibility setting.
- Ensure animations never prevent users from accessing content.

## 8. Responsive Design

The entire website must be responsive across:

- Small mobile phones: 320px–374px.
- Standard mobile phones: 375px–480px.
- Large phones: 481px–767px.
- Tablets: 768px–1023px.
- Laptops: 1024px–1439px.
- Large desktop screens: 1440px and above.

Requirements:
- No horizontal overflow.
- No clipped headings or buttons.
- Property grids adapt smoothly to available width.
- Images maintain suitable aspect ratios.
- Forms remain easy to complete on mobile.
- Navigation transforms into an accessible mobile menu.
- Text remains readable at every breakpoint.
- Touch targets are appropriately sized.
- Mobile layouts must be intentionally designed, not simply squeezed desktop layouts.

Test the important layouts at 320px, 375px, 768px, 1024px, 1440px, and 1920px widths.
